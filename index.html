Mission

Making Earth Observation Accessible for Disaster Risk Management

EO-DREAM is an open-source framework designed to simplify the use of satellite imagery for Disaster Risk Management. Through standardized, reproducible workflows and modular hazard analysis, EO-DREAM enables students, researchers, and operational agencies to transform Earth Observation data into actionable information.

Modules

Hazard Analysis Modules

EO-DREAM is designed around independent hazard modules that share a common processing workflow. New modules can be added without changing the EO-DREAM Core.

🌊 Flood

Detection of flooded areas using optical and radar imagery, including change analysis and affected area statistics.

🔥 Wildfire
